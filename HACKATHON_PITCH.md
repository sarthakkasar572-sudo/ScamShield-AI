# ScamShield AI — 3–5 Minute Pitch

## 1. Opening
“People don't need another red warning that says *phishing*. They need to know **why**, **what to do next**, and **how to recover** if they already made a mistake.”

## 2. Product
**ScamShield AI — Detect. Understand. Protect.**
A defensive, explainable personal digital-safety assistant for messages, URLs, screenshots, emails and QR codes.

## 3. Demo
1. Paste a fake prize/job message.
2. Show a transparent risk score and threat category.
3. Expand “Why we flagged it”.
4. Analyze the embedded URL without opening it.
5. Upload a screenshot to demonstrate OCR → analysis.
6. Select “I entered my password” under Incident Response.
7. Show the immediate recovery checklist.
8. Open the dashboard and show scan/threat counts.
9. Switch interface language to Hindi/Marathi.

## 4. Innovation
**DETECT → EXPLAIN → PROTECT → RECOVER → LEARN**

- Explainable rather than black-box warnings.
- Pre-interaction and post-incident assistance.
- Non-technical language for students, elderly users and underserved users.
- Multilingual safety education.
- Hybrid architecture: deterministic security signals + AI-ready explanation layer.

## 5. Safety
- No credential collection.
- No exploit generation or unauthorized scanning.
- No automatic URL opening.
- Uploaded files are validated and never executed.
- Risk is explicitly described as an automated indicator, not proof.

## 6. Scalability
The same API contracts can connect to approved threat-intelligence providers, enterprise email gateways, mobile clients and additional language models without changing the user-facing safety workflow.
